What you get

🔗 Website reviews & benchmarks

Link several websites, each labelled My restaurant or Benchmark. Malcolm reads each site and its menu pages. For your restaurant, he reports on cuisine, style, price point, strengths and gaps. For benchmarks, he shows where you can differentiate and suggests original ideas. He never copies their menus.

📄 Sample menu import

Use a menu from any linked website, upload a sample menu (a text-based PDF or text file, with photo menus on a best-effort basis), or paste one in. It can be yours or a benchmark. Malcolm develops your own menus further and treats benchmark menus as inspiration only.

📚 Cookbook benchmarks

Upload recipe books by any chef for private reference. You’ll need to confirm you own the book or have the right to use it. Uploads stay private to your account: they’re never shared, never made public and never used for anyone else, and you can delete them at any time. Malcolm studies only the style, techniques, flavour pairings, structure and themes. He never reproduces recipes or text in menus, PDFs or share links, and he credits each book as inspiration.

🧭 Smart recipe search

Pick dishes from your own account history and favourites. Filter and sort by appeal, variety, taste profile, dietary needs, season and food-cost target.

🍽️ Menu Builder

Set your venue type (restaurant, pub or gastropub, bar, café, hotel and more) and description, then choose the menu type. Restaurant formats include à la carte, tasting, set, prix fixe, banquet, small plates, brunch and private dining. Pub formats include pub classics, bar snacks, Sunday roast, a specials board, set lunch, a seasonal carvery and sharing platters. Then set the cuisines and style, the number of courses and the dishes per course.

✨ Intelligent refinements

Choose the appeal (broad, adventurous, classic or family), the variety and the taste balance. Add dietary cover in every course, the season, a food-cost target, suggested prices, drink pairings (wine, ale or cider, as suggestions only) and your own personal brief.

📖 Every dish, a full recipe

Each dish on a generated menu opens its full recipe, with ingredients, method, timings, yield, UK-14 allergens, food-safety notes and Health Benefits where they apply. Recipes are saved with the menu and in your account history.

💷 Budget & pricing tiers

Set ingredient sourcing (economy to luxury), target food cost %, cost-per-cover cap, customer pricing tier and GP target. Malcolm adapts dishes and shows estimated food cost and suggested selling price per dish (GBP, inc. VAT, clearly marked estimates).

👩‍🍳 Brigade-aware Kitchen Pack

Tell Malcolm how many chefs are on, their roles, covers, prep time and equipment. Menus scale to your team, and the Kitchen Pack adds costing, station delegation, a mise en place schedule and service pick-up timings alongside full recipes.

📰 Restauranteur Weekly

A personalised briefing every Monday: seasonal produce, specials, menu ideas, trends and cost-saving tips for your venue (other plans get the monthly magazine).

🖨️ Save, share & export

Every menu is saved to your account. Share it with your team through a private link, or download a print-ready A4 PDF in classic fine-dining, pub chalkboard or rustic kraft style, designed around your venue’s details. You can add an optional Kitchen pack of full recipe and prep sheets for every dish, with clickable recipe links. Guest pages stay clean.
