Malcolm, your AI chef assistant

Malcolm is an AI. Answers may be wrong: check allergen labels, cook to safe core temperatures; nutrition is an estimate; not medical advice.

With your explicit consent (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(a), 9(2)(a)) we store your kitchen profile (allergies, diets, foods to avoid, kit, skill, household size, favourites, voice preference) to personalise suggestions: with your account if signed in, otherwise only in your browser. Voice uses your browser’s speech features (your browser vendor may process audio); we only receive the text. “Contact Oliver” messages are kept up to 90 days. Contact: malcolm@aichef.food.

Malcolm’s personal memory (optional)

Only if you are signed in and switch it on, Malcolm can remember: (a) your conversations with him; (b) your recipe searches, from which we work out favourite cuisines, ingredients, dietary needs, usual cooking time and servings; and (c) first-party signals of how you use AIchef.food (pages and recipes you view or save, and use of the Menu Builder and Kitchen Pack). Each is a separate choice, all are off by default, and nothing is collected before you choose or while you are signed out.

Lawful basis: your consent (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)); where dietary needs could reveal health or religious information, your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)). Browsing signals are only stored with your consent, as PECR requires. We record when you consented and which version of this notice you agreed to.

What we never do: no third-party trackers or advertising, no selling or sharing, no IP address, device fingerprinting or cross-site tracking, and no use of your data for any other member. Staff see only anonymous totals, never your chats or searches.

Use: a short summary of your profile and relevant recent messages is added to what Malcolm sees when you chat, so his replies fit you. Chat content is processed by our AI providers acting as our processors: the Malcolm AI service (run by us on Cloudflare), which passes it to Google (Gemini API) or Cloudflare Workers AI to generate the reply, and Cloudflare Workers AI directly as our fallback.

Retention: up to 12 months, then deleted automatically. Switching a choice off deletes what it collected.

Your controls: on What Malcolm remembers you can view everything, download it as JSON, pause it, or delete it all at any time. Deleting your account deletes it too. Clear History on your account page also wipes your search history, saved preferences, saved menus and kitchen packs, Malcolm’s memory and kitchen profile, recipe view counts and past free magazine previews in one go, while keeping your account, plan, household, consent choices, ratings and (unless you tick the box) favourites. Contact: malcolm@aichef.food.

Changing your email and magazine delivery

You can edit your name and change your account email on your account page. To change your email we ask for your current password, send a confirmation link to the new address and only switch once you click it; we then tell your old address about the change and sign out your other devices. If you pay us, we update the email on your Stripe billing record too. Magazine subscribers can have their magazine delivered to a different address; we email that address a confirmation link and only send magazines there once it is confirmed. You can switch back to your account email at any time. Confirmation links are single-use and expire after 24 hours, and we store only a scrambled (hashed) copy of them.
