FAQ

Questions, answered

Who is Malcolm?

Malcolm is the AI chef behind AIchef.food. He turns your ingredients, time, skill and diet into complete recipes, using Escoffier's classical guide as his reference.

Is it really free to start?

Yes. Commis Chef is free for ever with 3 recipe searches, and new accounts get a 7-day Head Chef trial with no card needed.

What's included in the monthly magazine?

Three personalised recipes, wine pairings, an aisle-grouped shopping list and an Escoffier note, delivered as a PDF. It's included in Head Chef and Gastronomique, or available as an add-on.

Can my whole household use one plan?

Gastronomique · Lux Prime covers up to 5 members. Each gets their own account and their own personalised magazine.

Are the dish photos real?

They're photoreal previews created by Malcolm's image engine to show roughly how the dish will look. Your plate may differ.

How do you handle allergies and diets?

Recipes are tagged for the UK 14 allergens, and you can filter for vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free and halal. Always check product labels.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. You can manage or cancel your plan from your account at any time.
