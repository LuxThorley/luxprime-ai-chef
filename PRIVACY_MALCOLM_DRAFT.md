# Draft privacy notice addition: "Malcolm, your AI chef assistant" (for Oliver's approval)

**Malcolm is an AI.** The "Ask Malcolm" assistant on every page is an automated AI system. Its answers can be wrong. Always check allergen labels, cook food to safe core temperatures, and treat nutrition figures as estimates. Malcolm does not give medical advice.

**What we store, and only with your consent.** If you tick the consent box under "My kitchen", we store your kitchen profile: allergies, diets, foods to avoid, kitchen equipment, skill level, household size, favourite foods and voice preference. Allergy and diet information can reveal health or religious information. We process it only on the basis of your explicit consent (UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a)), and only to personalise Malcolm's suggestions. Signed-in members' profiles are kept with your account. If you're not signed in, the profile stays in your browser only.

**Chats.** Your messages go to our server to produce a reply. They may be processed by Cloudflare Workers AI, our processor. If you send a fridge photo, it is analysed once to list the ingredients and is not stored. Your chat history is kept in your browser. Rate-limit counters are kept for up to 24 hours.

**Voice.** Speech recognition and read-aloud use your browser's built-in speech features. Depending on your browser, the audio may be processed by your browser vendor (for example Google or Apple). We only receive the recognised text.

**Messages to Oliver.** If you use "Contact Oliver", we keep your message and email for up to 90 days so we can reply.

**Your rights.** You can view, change or delete your kitchen profile at any time under "My kitchen" → "Delete my data". Deleting it also clears the chat history on that device. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect anything processed before you withdrew it. Contact: malcolm@aichef.food.
